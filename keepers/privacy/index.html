Keepers Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Keepers helps you find and remove duplicate photos. It was built so that your photos stay yours.

What Keepers accesses

  • Your photo library, with the permission you grant. Keepers reads photos to compare them and find near-duplicates. When you confirm a deletion, it asks Photos to delete the photos you chose, and they go to Recently Deleted.
  • Photo details such as capture date, dimensions, and camera and lens model. These are used for grouping and filtering.

What stays on your device

Everything. Photos are analysed on your iPhone or iPad. Keepers saves the analysis it creates (image fingerprints, groups, and your keep/delete choices) in the app's own storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Keepers has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Purchases

The optional unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. Keepers never receives your payment details. A count of free deletions used is stored in the device's Keychain.

Crash and performance data

If you've chosen to share analytics with developers in iOS Settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash and performance reports. They contain no photos or personal information.

Contacting support

If you email support, we receive what you send, including the app version, iOS version, and device model that Keepers adds to the draft. You can remove those before sending. We use your email only to reply to you.

Children

Keepers doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

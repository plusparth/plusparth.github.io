Common questions

Is anything deleted before I confirm?

No. Marking a photo only stages it. Nothing is deleted until you tap Delete in Final Review and confirm in the system prompt.

I deleted the wrong photo.

Deleted photos go to Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted for 30 days. Open the photo and tap Recover.

What's free?

Scanning and reviewing are unlimited. The first 20 deletions are free. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks unlimited deletions. There's no subscription.

I bought the unlock but Keepers still shows the limit.

Open Settings → Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought with. Family Sharing members get the unlock too.

Keepers says some photos can't be deleted.

Photos in shared albums or synced from a computer can't be deleted by apps. Mark them Keep, then delete the rest.

The scan is slow or stopped.

The first scan analyses every photo in the range you picked. You can review groups while it runs, pause it, or leave the app. It picks up where it left off. Photos stored only in iCloud take longer.

Does Keepers upload my photos?

No. Everything happens on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
